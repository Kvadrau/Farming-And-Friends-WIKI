Animal Licenses ​

Animal Licenses unlock animals, factories, and upgrade paths related to animal production. Licenses are upgraded using License XP earned from Animal License tasks.

📈 License Progression ​

LicenseXP RequiredDescription
🍳 Scrambled Eggs3.6k XPTakes eggs and scrambles them.
🧀 Cheese Factory7.2k XPProduce more with your Milk! Unlock a factory for Cheese production!
🐝 Bees7.2k XPBe careful! This license will allow you to keep BEES! Harvest the hives and refine them into honey.
🐑 Sheep7.2k XPNeed a fluffier animal? Getting sheep is the way to go. Use a spindle to refine their wool.
🐄 Cow Efficiency7.2k XPMore milk.
🐔 Chicken Efficiency7.2k XPMore eggs.
📦 Animal Food Efficiency7.2k XPMakes animal food.
🍕 Pizza Factory14.4k XPMakes Pizza.
🥣 Honey Oatmeal14.4k XPWant to do more with your Honey? This factory produces Honey Oatmeal!
👕 Shirt Factory14.4k XPMake use of your wool bundles! Build a Shirt Factory for greater profits!
🎂 Cake Factory Efficiency14.4k XPMore cake batter.

📝 Notes ​

  • Most factory-related licenses unlock five levels of efficiency upgrades.
  • Cow Efficiency and Chicken Efficiency contain four upgrade levels.
  • Bees is the only currently documented Animal License that unlocks multiple upgrade categories.
  • The Cake Factory produces Cake Batter, which must then be processed in the Oven that is included with the Cake Factory unlock.
