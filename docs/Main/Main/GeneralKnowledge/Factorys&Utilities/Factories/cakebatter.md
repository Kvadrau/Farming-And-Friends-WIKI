# Cake Factory

The **Cake Factory** is used to combine Wheat, Eggs, and Milk to produce **Cake Batter**. The Cake Batter can then be placed into an **Oven Building** to produce finished Cakes.

## Factory Information

| Information            | Details                                                                                          |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Purchase price**     | 45,000 Coins                                                                                     |
| **Required materials** | Wheat, Eggs and Milk                                                                             |
| **Production time**    | Requires multiple lever stages; exact processing time is not currently documented                |
| **Products produced**  | Cake Batter                                                                                      |
| **Selling price**      | Cake Batter itself is not the final product. Finished Cake sells for approximately 8,000 Coins   |
| **Required equipment** | Suitable vehicle or trailer for transporting Cake Batter; Oven Building required to produce Cake |

The Cake Factory costs **45,000 Coins**, has storage for **50 Milk, 50 Eggs and 5,000 Wheat**, and has a building size of **7 × 10**.

## Required Materials

One Cake Batter requires:

| Material        | Amount |
| --------------- | -----: |
| 🌾 Wheat        |     50 |
| 🥚 Eggs         |  1 Box |
| 🥛 Regular Milk |  1 Jug |

Free-Range Milk can also be used. One jug of Free-Range Milk counts as two jugs of Regular Milk. Spoiled Milk cannot be used.

The factory can produce up to **6 boxes of Cake Batter at a time**.

## Production Time

The exact processing time of the Cake Factory is not currently documented as a fixed number.

Production requires the player to manually operate a sequence of levers inside the factory. Each lever requires a short waiting period before the next lever becomes available.

> **Note:** The production time listed here should not be confused with the baking time. Once Cake Batter has been produced, it must be placed into an Oven Building.

## Products Produced

The Cake Factory produces **Cake Batter**.

Cake Batter is not the finished Cake. To turn Cake Batter into Cake, it must be placed into an **Oven Building**.

The Oven Building contains **8 ovens**, allowing up to 8 Cakes to be baked simultaneously. Current documentation lists the baking time as approximately **10 minutes**.

## Selling Price

The finished **Cake** can be sold at AnimalCo for approximately **8,000 Coins** per Cake.

Cake Batter itself is an intermediate product and is therefore not the final item in the Cake production chain.

## Required Equipment

The Cake production chain requires:

* **Cake Factory** — Produces Cake Batter.
* **Oven Building** — Converts Cake Batter into Cake.
* **Suitable transport** — Used to transport ingredients and collect finished Cake Batter.
* **Chicken setup** — Required to produce Eggs.
* **Cow setup** — Required to produce Milk.
* **Wheat fields** — Required to produce Wheat.

## How to Use the Factory

### 1. Load the Ingredients

Bring the required ingredients to the Cake Factory.

For at least one Cake Batter, you need:

* **50 Wheat**
* **1 Box of Eggs**
* **1 Jug of Regular Milk**

Wheat and Milk can be deposited on the left side of the building.

Eggs are deposited inside the building onto the conveyor belt.

### 2. Start Production

Enter the Cake Factory and locate the first lever.

When enough ingredients have been supplied, the lever will display:

> **Pull Lever to Begin**

Pull the lever to start production.

If the message does not appear, the factory does not have enough ingredients to produce at least one batch.

### 3. Follow the Lever Sequence

After pulling the first lever, wait for the next lever to become available.

The sequence moves through several levers:

1. First lever
2. Lever opposite the first
3. Lever below the conveyor belt
4. Final lever upstairs on the right

Pull each lever when its prompt becomes available.

### 4. Collect the Cake Batter

Once production is complete, the Cake Batter is delivered to the loading area on the right side of the building.

If a suitable vehicle or trailer is positioned at the loading area, the Cake Batter will automatically load onto it.

### 5. Bake the Cake

Take the Cake Batter to an **Oven Building**.

Place the Cake Batter onto the storage tray on the right side of the entrance.

The Oven Building will then begin baking the Cake. Current documentation states that baking takes approximately **10 minutes**.

Once finished, the Cakes are automatically placed onto the storage rack on the left side of the Oven Building.

---

> **Note:** Cake production requires several stages, from growing Wheat and producing Eggs and Milk, to making Cake Batter and finally baking it. Make sure you have both a Cake Factory and an Oven Building before starting the full production chain.
