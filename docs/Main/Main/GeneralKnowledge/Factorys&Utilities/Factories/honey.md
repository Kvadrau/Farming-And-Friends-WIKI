# Honey

Honey is an animal product produced by **Bees**. Bees are placed inside Beehives, where they produce Honey Frames that can be processed by the **Honey Factory** into Honey Barrels and Honey Pallets.

Honey is primarily a secondary income source and can also be used to produce **Honey-Oatmeal**.

## Honey Information

| Information         | Details       |
| ------------------- | ------------- |
| Main Source         | Bees          |
| Production Building | Honey Factory |
| Factory Price       | 80,000 Coins  |
| Required License    | Bee License   |
| Product             | Honey         |
| Pallet Size         | 4 Barrels     |
| Frames per Barrel   | 6             |
| Frames per Pallet   | 24            |

The Honey Factory converts Honey Frames produced by bees into Honey Barrels, with **4 Barrels making up one Honey Pallet**.

## Required Equipment

To begin producing Honey, you will need:

* **Bee License**
* **Beehive**
* **Bees**
* **Honey Factory**
* **Forklift**
* **Pallet-capable vehicle or trailer**

Honey is stored as pallets rather than a liquid product, so a forklift and suitable transport are important for moving finished Honey.

## Bee Production

Bees produce Honey Frames while inside a Beehive.

| Information        | Details    |
| ------------------ | ---------- |
| Honey per Bee      | 4 Frames   |
| Production Time    | 15 Minutes |
| Frames per Barrel  | 6          |
| Frames per Pallet  | 24         |
| Barrels per Pallet | 4          |

For example, **3 Bees** producing 4 frames each will create:

**3 × 4 = 12 Honey Frames**

Those 12 frames are enough to produce **2 Honey Barrels**.

## How to Produce Honey

### 1. Unlock the Bee License

The Bee License is required before you can begin using the bee production system.

Bee-related progression is tied to the **Bee Goals branch**.

### 2. Purchase a Beehive

Purchase and place a Beehive on your farm.

A hive can house multiple Bees, with the starting capacity being **2 Bees per hive** according to the available documentation. Hive capacity can be increased through progression.

### 3. Purchase Bees

Bees can be purchased from **AnimalCo**.

Each Bee is placed inside a Beehive and begins producing Honey Frames. Current community documentation lists a Bee Box at **15,000 Coins**.

### 4. Wait for the Bees

Bees take approximately **15 minutes** to produce Honey.

Each Bee produces **4 Honey Frames** during a production cycle.

### 5. Collect the Honey Frames

Once the Bees have finished producing, collect the Honey Frames from the Beehive.

These frames are the raw material required by the Honey Factory.

### 6. Process the Honey

Take the Honey Frames to the Honey Factory.

The conversion is:

**6 Honey Frames → 1 Honey Barrel**

A full Honey Pallet requires:

**24 Honey Frames → 4 Honey Barrels → 1 Honey Pallet**

### 7. Transport the Honey

Honey is stored as pallets, so use a **Forklift** to move the pallet onto a suitable flatbed vehicle or trailer.

Compatible vehicles include options such as the **M2 Flatbed, M1 Flatbed, R350 Flatbed and Flatbed Hauler S-500**.

### 8. Sell the Honey

Honey can be sold through the animal-product selling system at **AnimalCo**.

The older documented maximum was **30,000 Coins per Honey Pallet**, or **7,500 Coins per Barrel**. However, this price is explicitly marked as outdated in the source, so the current in-game selling price should be used instead.

## Honey-Oatmeal

Honey can also be used to produce **Honey-Oatmeal**.

The Honey-Oatmeal Factory uses **1 Honey Pallet and harvested Oats** to produce Honey-Oatmeal. A full Honey-Oatmeal pallet contains 27 bundles and has historically been listed at up to **87,750 Coins**.

```text
Honey Pallet ─────┐
                  ↓
              Oatmeal
                  ↑
                  │
Oats ─────────────┘
```

> **Note:** Honey is generally considered a secondary or specialised income source rather than a primary money-making method. It is particularly useful when progressing through Bee Goals or when combining Honey with Oats for Honey-Oatmeal.
