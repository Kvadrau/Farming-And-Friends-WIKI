 # Chocolate Milk Factory

The Chocolate Milk Factory is a production building that combines **Cocoa** and **Milk** to produce Chocolate Milk.

Chocolate Milk is considered a high-value production chain, but requires several unlocks and pieces of equipment before it can be used efficiently.

## Factory Information

| Information      | Details                |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| Purchase Price   | 50,000 Coins           |
| Main Inputs      | Cocoa & Milk           |
| Product          | Chocolate Milk         |
| Maximum Output   | 60 Jugs                |
| Required License | Chocolate Milk License |
| Build Menu       | Create > Buildings     |

The Chocolate Milk Factory costs **50,000 Coins** and can produce up to **60 Chocolate Milk Jugs** when fully supplied.

## Required Materials

Each Chocolate Milk Jug requires:

| Material     | Amount  |
| ------------ | ------- |
| Cocoa        | 25      |
| Regular Milk | 10 Jugs |

Alternatively, Free-Range Milk can be used:

| Material        | Amount |
| --------------- | ------ |
| Cocoa           | 25     |
| Free-Range Milk | 5 Jugs |

Free-Range Milk counts as **2 Regular Milk**, meaning only 5 Free-Range Milk is required for one Chocolate Milk Jug.

### Full Production Batch

For the maximum **60 Chocolate Milk Jugs**, you will need:

| Material     | Amount   |
| ------------ | -------- |
| Cocoa        | 1,500    |
| Regular Milk | 600 Jugs |

Or:

| Material        | Amount   |
| --------------- | -------- |
| Cocoa           | 1,500    |
| Free-Range Milk | 300 Jugs |

## Production Time

A fixed production time for the Chocolate Milk Factory is not currently documented reliably.

Production requires the player to manually operate the factory's lever sequence after loading the required materials.

> **Note:** The exact processing time should be confirmed in-game before being added as a fixed value.

## Products Produced

The Chocolate Milk Factory produces:

| Product            | Maximum Amount |
| ------------------ | -------------- |
| Chocolate Milk Jug | 60             |

Each jug requires **25 Cocoa and 10 Regular Milk**, or **25 Cocoa and 5 Free-Range Milk**.

## Selling Price

Chocolate Milk currently sells for approximately **20,000 Coins per Jug** according to the June 2026 community guide.

At approximately 20,000 Coins per jug:

**60 × 20,000 = 1,200,000 Coins**

A full production batch could therefore be worth approximately **1.2 million Coins** before considering costs and current market/selling values.

> **Note:** Selling prices can change, so the in-game selling price should be treated as the final authority.

## Required Equipment

To operate a Chocolate Milk Factory, you will need:

* **Chocolate Milk Factory**
* **Chocolate Milk License**
* **Cocoa Trees**
* **Cocoa Harvester**
* **Forest Soil**
* **Cows**
* **Milk production setup**
* **Milk storage/transport**
* **Suitable trailer or transport vehicle**

The **Oxbo 3220** is listed at approximately **140,000 Coins** and can be used to harvest Cocoa. The Oxbo 4220 is another compatible option.

Cocoa itself requires the **Chocolate Milk License**, with the Forestry License required first.

## How to Use the Factory

### 1. Unlock the Chocolate Milk License

Before producing Chocolate Milk, you need to unlock the required **Chocolate Milk License** through the game's Goals progression.

The Forestry License is required before the Chocolate Milk License.

### 2. Set Up Cocoa

Cocoa Trees are grown on **Forest Soil**.

Cocoa requires a compatible harvester such as the **Oxbo 3220** or **Oxbo 4220** to collect the Cocoa.

### 3. Produce Milk

You will also need a supply of milk from cows.

Regular Milk can be used directly, while Free-Range Milk provides a 2:1 conversion and can reduce the amount of milk required per jug.

### 4. Load the Factory

Place the required Cocoa and Milk onto the elevated loading platform on the factory.

The minimum recipe is:

**25 Cocoa + 10 Regular Milk → 1 Chocolate Milk Jug**

or:

**25 Cocoa + 5 Free-Range Milk → 1 Chocolate Milk Jug**

The factory must have enough ingredients for at least one complete recipe before production can begin.

### 5. Start Production

Enter the factory and activate the first lever.

Continue following the prompts and pull the remaining levers in the required sequence.

> **Important:** Skipping a lever or pulling them out of order can interrupt the production process.

### 6. Collect the Chocolate Milk

Once production has finished, collect the Chocolate Milk Jugs from the output area.

A fully supplied factory can produce up to **60 Jugs** in one batch.

### 7. Transport and Sell

Load the Chocolate Milk onto a suitable trailer or transport vehicle and take it to a selling location.

At approximately **20,000 Coins per Jug**, Chocolate Milk can provide a significant return when produced at scale.

## Costs to Consider

The Chocolate Milk Factory itself costs **50,000 Coins**, but the complete production setup is considerably more expensive.

A basic setup may also require:

| Item                   | Approximate Cost               |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Chocolate Milk Factory | 50,000 Coins                   |
| Oxbo 3220              | 140,000 Coins                  |
| Cocoa Seeds            | 70,000 Coins per 50-tree batch |
| Cows & Barns           | Varies                         |
| Trailer                | Varies                         |
| Forest Soil            | Varies                         |

The community guide estimates a minimum setup of around **260,000 Coins** before additional barns, cows, fencing and trailers are included.

> **Tip:** Chocolate Milk is generally more suited to mid-to-late game players because of the high initial investment and the number of licenses, animals, crops and vehicles required.
