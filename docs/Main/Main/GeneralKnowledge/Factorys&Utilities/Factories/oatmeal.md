# Honey Oatmeal

Honey Oatmeal is an advanced food product made using **Honey** and **Oats**. It is produced in the **Honey Oatmeal Factory** and can be sold for a significant amount of Coins.

## Product Information

| Information         | Details                 |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Production Building | Honey Oatmeal Factory   |
| Main Ingredients    | Honey & Oats            |
| Oats Required       | 100 Harvested Oats      |
| Honey Required      | 1 Honey Pallet          |
| Output              | Honey Oatmeal           |
| Pallet Size         | 27 Bundles              |
| Maximum Sell Value  | 87,750 Coins per Pallet |

A full pallet of Honey Oatmeal contains **27 bundles**. Each bundle can sell for up to **3,250 Coins**, giving a maximum pallet value of **87,750 Coins**.

## Required Materials

To produce one pallet of Honey Oatmeal, you need:

| Material       | Amount   |
| -------------- | -------- |
| Honey          | 1 Pallet |
| Harvested Oats | 100      |

One Honey Pallet contains **4 Honey Barrels**, which are consumed by the Honey Oatmeal Factory.

> **Note:** The factory may currently require **101 Oats to be loaded** because of a floating-point counter issue, although only 100 Oats are ultimately consumed.

## Production Time

A fixed production time for Honey Oatmeal is not reliably documented.

Production requires the player to supply the required Honey and Oats and operate the Honey Oatmeal Factory.

## Products Produced

The factory produces **Honey Oatmeal Bundles**.

| Product       | Amount                |
| ------------- | --------------------- |
| Honey Oatmeal | 27 Bundles per Pallet |

Honey Oatmeal is sold by the bundle rather than having a separate fixed pallet selling price.

## Selling Price

The maximum documented selling price is:

**3,250 Coins per Bundle**

A full pallet contains 27 bundles:

**27 × 3,250 = 87,750 Coins**

| Product    | Maximum Value |
| ---------- | ------------- |
| 1 Bundle   | 3,250 Coins   |
| 27 Bundles | 87,750 Coins  |

These are maximum documented values and may change with future updates.

## Required Equipment

To produce Honey Oatmeal, you will need:

* **Honey Oatmeal Factory**
* **Honey**
* **Honey Factory**
* **Beehives**
* **Bees**
* **Oat Seeds**
* **Farming equipment**
* **Suitable transport for Honey pallets**

Oats can be grown using any level of farming equipment and take approximately **8 minutes** to grow without growth multipliers.

## How to Make Honey Oatmeal

### 1. Unlock Honey Oatmeal

You must unlock the **Honey Oatmeal Farming Goal** before producing Honey Oatmeal. You also need a reliable source of Honey.

### 2. Produce Honey

Use Bees and Beehives to produce Honey Frames.

Process the Honey Frames through the **Honey Factory** to create Honey Barrels.

You need **4 Honey Barrels**, equivalent to **1 Honey Pallet**, for one Honey Oatmeal batch.

### 3. Grow Oats

Purchase Oat Seeds from FarmCo and plant them.

Oats take approximately **8 minutes** to fully grow without any growth-speed multipliers.

### 4. Harvest the Oats

Harvest the fully grown Oats using your farming equipment.

You need **100 harvested Oats** for one Honey Oatmeal pallet.

### 5. Load the Honey Oatmeal Factory

Place the required materials into the Honey Oatmeal Factory:

**1 Honey Pallet + 100 Harvested Oats**

The Honey counter represents Honey Barrels, so one Honey Pallet appears as **4 Barrels**.

### 6. Start Production

Once the required materials have been supplied, operate the factory to begin production.

### 7. Collect the Honey Oatmeal

The finished product is produced as **Honey Oatmeal Bundles**.

A full pallet contains **27 bundles**.

### 8. Sell the Honey Oatmeal

Take the finished Honey Oatmeal to the appropriate selling location.

At the maximum documented price:

**27 Bundles × 3,250 Coins = 87,750 Coins**.

> **Tip:** Honey Oatmeal is an advanced production chain because it requires both a Honey production setup and an Oat farming setup. It is therefore better suited to players who already have access to Bees, Honey production, and efficient crop farming.
