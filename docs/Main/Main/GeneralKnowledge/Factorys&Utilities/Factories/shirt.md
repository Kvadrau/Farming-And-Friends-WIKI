# SHIRT FACTORY 


Shirt Boxes are an animal derived product produced from Sheep using Wool Bundles. 
Sheep  are placed inside fence, given food, and adequate space to grow Wool on their body. 
Wool is sheared by standing close to the sheep and clicking on shear. 
Shears as an equipment is NOT required. 

> Shirts are the the final product that can be made from sheeps. It can be sold or fulfilled in company related tasks.

## Required Equipment 
To operate and transport Shirts efficiently, you will need: 

- Sheep for producing wool
- Spindle Machine for producing wool bundles
- Transport Equipment 
- Flatbed Hauler S-500 or any other pallet-capable vehicle 
- Forklift for handling Shirt pallets 

A suitable pallet-capable vehicle and forklift are required to efficiently transport Shirt Pallets. 

## How to Use the Shirt Factory 

### 1. Purchase the Shirt Factory 

Purchase the Cheese Factory for 70,000 coins and place it on your farm. 

### 2. Produce Wool 
You will need Sheep to steady supply of sheer wool. 

Sheep can be sheared by standing near to a sheep with wool 100%, once sheered gives 1 wool per sheep 
Note: without any sheep efficiency 

### 3. Place Spindle Machine 
Place the Spindle Machine for 5000 coins 
Load the Spindle Machine with 6 sheer wool at one time 
3 sheer wool yields 1 wool bundle. 

### 4. Load the Shirt Factory 
Load the wool bundles from spindle machine onto the input of shirt factory 
1 wool bundles -> 2 shirt boxes 

### 5. Collect the Shirt Box Pallet 
Produced shirts are stored as boxes and eventually forms pallets. 
A complete pallet contains 27 shirt boxes. 

### 6. Transport the Pallet 
Once a pallet is ready, use a suitable pallet-capable vehicle such as the Flatbed Hauler S-500, and a Forklift to move it. 

### 7. Sell the Shirt Box
Take the finished Shirt box to Animal Co to sell it.
