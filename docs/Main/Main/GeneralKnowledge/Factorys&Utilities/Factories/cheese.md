# Cheese Factory

The Cheese Factory is a production building that converts milk into cheese. Cheese is primarily used for selling at AnimalCo or as an ingredient for producing Pizza.

## Factory Information

| Information | Details |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Purchase Price | 75,000 Coins |
| Storage | Unlimited |
| Main Input | Regular Milk / Free-Range Milk |
| Product | Cheese |
| Output | Cheese Boxes / Pallets |
| Selling Location | AnimalCo |

The Cheese Factory costs **75,000 Coins** and is used to turn milk into cheese. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Required Materials

The Cheese Factory uses milk as its only ingredient.

| Material | Amount |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Regular Milk | 3 Jugs |
| Cheese Produced | 2 Boxes |

This means a full pallet of **27 Cheese Boxes** requires **54 jugs of regular milk**.

Free-Range Milk counts as **2 Regular Milk** when placed into the factory, meaning you need **27 Free-Range Milk** for a full pallet. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Production Time

The Cheese Factory does not currently have a reliably documented fixed production time in the available community documentation.

Production is handled through the factory's internal production process rather than a simple crop-style growth timer.

> **Note:** Production time may vary depending on the current version of the game. This section should be updated if an exact in-game production time is confirmed.

## Products Produced

The Cheese Factory produces **Cheese Boxes**.

A full pallet contains:

| Product | Amount |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Cheese | 27 Boxes |

Cheese is produced in batches of **2 boxes from 3 regular milk**. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Selling Price

Cheese can be sold at **AnimalCo**.

The current community documentation lists the maximum price at approximately **1,311 Coins per Cheese Box**.

A full pallet of 27 boxes could therefore sell for approximately:

**27 × 1,311 = 35,397 Coins**

The listed price is a per-box value, not the value of the entire pallet. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

> **Note:** Selling prices can change with game updates, so verify the current in-game price before treating this as a permanent value.

## Required Equipment

To operate and transport Cheese efficiently, you will need:

- **Cheese Factory**
- **Cows** for producing milk
- **Milk storage/transport equipment**
- **Flatbed Hauler S-500** or another pallet-capable vehicle
- **Forklift** for handling Cheese pallets

A suitable pallet-capable vehicle and forklift are required to efficiently transport Cheese pallets. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

## How to Use the Cheese Factory

### 1. Purchase the Factory

Purchase the **Cheese Factory** for **75,000 Coins** and place it on your farm.

### 2. Produce Milk

You will need a steady supply of milk from your cows.

Regular Milk can be used directly. Free-Range Milk counts as **2 Regular Milk**, making it more efficient for the factory. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

### 3. Load the Milk

Transport your milk to the Cheese Factory and place it into the factory's input storage.

The factory's milk counter measures the amount of **Regular Milk** available.

### 4. Start Production

Once enough milk is available, begin the factory's production process.

The factory converts:

**3 Regular Milk → 2 Cheese**

### 5. Collect the Cheese

Produced Cheese is stored as boxes and eventually forms pallets.

A complete pallet contains **27 Cheese Boxes**.

### 6. Transport the Pallet

Once a pallet is ready, use a suitable pallet-capable vehicle such as the **Flatbed Hauler S-500** and a **Forklift** to move it. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

### 7. Sell the Cheese

Take the finished Cheese to **AnimalCo** to sell it.
