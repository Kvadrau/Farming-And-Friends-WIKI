# AnimalCo

**AnimalCo** is the main shop for purchasing animals, animal food, and bee boxes in **Farming And Friends**.

| Type         |  Price |
| ------------ | -----------: |
| 🌾Animal Food    |     $450 |
| 🐝 Bees      |     $15,000 |



## How to buy Animal Food & Bees

To purchase animal food or bees:

1. Go to AnimalCo and enter the store
2. Pick up the items you want to purchase from the shelves
<br>

3. Place the items on the counter.
4. Click Purchase.
5. Once purchased, load the boxes into your truck or trailer and take them back to your farm.

::: tip
If you leave the game after purchasing items, your boxes will appear at the front of your farm or inside a garage
:::

## How to Get Animals

To purchase and transport animals from AnimalCo, you will need a **truck** and an **animal trailer**.

### 1. Find AnimalCo

First, travel to **AnimalCo** and make sure you have your truck and animal trailer with you.
![animalco](/images/animal1.png)

### 2. Go to AnimalCo Pickup

Drive into the fenced-in **AnimalCo Pickup** area and stop your animal trailer inside the designated area.
![animalco](/images/animal2.png)

The animal purchase menu should appear once you are in the correct location.

### 3. Purchase Your Animals

Choose the animal you want to purchase and select the quantity you want.

Once you are happy with your selection, click **Purchase**.

The animals will then be loaded into your animal trailer.
<br>

### 4. Return to Your Farm

Drive your truck and animal trailer back to your farm.

Position the animal trailer over the entrance of the fenced animal enclosure.

A **yellow-lined grid** will show you where the trailer should be positioned.
![animalco](/images/animal4.png)
![animalco](/images/animal5.png)

Make sure the trailer is positioned correctly over the enclosure entrance. This allows you to unload the animals directly into the enclosure without having to enter it.
