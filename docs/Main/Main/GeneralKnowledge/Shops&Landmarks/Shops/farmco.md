# FarmCo

**FarmCo** is a shop in **Farming And Friends** where players can purchase crop seeds and crop pallets.

::: tip
🍇 Grape Seed Boxes: Unlike the other crop boxes sold by FarmCo, Grape Seed Boxes contain only 10 seeds per box. All other crop boxes contain 50 seeds per box.
:::

## Pallet Information

* Each pallet contains **27 crop boxes**.
* Pallets are sold at approximately a **5% discount** compared to purchasing 27 individual crop boxes.
* A **forklift** is required to move the pallets.
