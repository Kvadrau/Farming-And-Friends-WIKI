# Company Orders

Company Orders are tasks that Companies complete to progress through the Companies system.

Orders require Company members to gather the required materials, fulfil the order, and prepare it for shipment.

Completing Orders is one of the main ways for a Company to progress and work towards larger Company rewards and equipment.

## How Orders Work

Company Orders follow a simple process:

1. View the available Company Orders.
2. Select an Order for the Company to complete.
3. Review the required Materials.
4. Gather the required Materials.
5. Fulfil the Order.
6. Prepare the completed Order for shipment.
7. Ship the Order.
8. Receive the associated Company progression and rewards.
