# Company Materials

Company Materials are resources used to complete **Company Orders**.

Materials must be gathered, produced, or otherwise obtained by Company members before they can be used to fulfil Orders.

## How Materials Work

Company Orders require specific Materials in specific quantities.

The general process is:

1. Check the current Company Order.
2. Identify the required Materials.
3. Gather or produce the required Materials.
4. Deliver the Materials to the Company.
5. Use them to fulfil the Order.
6. Prepare the completed Order for shipment.

      ↓
