# Company Train

The **Company Train** is part of the Companies system and is used to obtain and transport materials for Company activities.

The train provides Companies with another way to obtain resources that can be used when completing Company Orders.

## Purpose

The Company Train is primarily used to obtain materials for the Company.

Materials obtained through the train can be used towards Company Orders and progression.

The train is particularly useful when a Company needs materials that are not immediately available through its normal farming, animal, forestry, or production activities.

## How to Use

To use the Company Train:

1. Access the Company Train.
2. Review the available materials.
3. Select the required material.
4. Purchase or request the material using the available Company resources.
5. Wait for the train to arrive or complete its delivery.
6. Collect the materials.
7. Use the materials for Company activities and Orders.

> **Note:** The materials and options available through the Company Train may change depending on the current Company Season.

## Materials

The Company Train can provide materials that can be used by the Company.

The available materials depend on the current train inventory.

| Material | Availability |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Company Materials | Varies |
| Seasonal Materials | May vary by Season |

Materials obtained through the train can be used when fulfilling Company Orders.

See [Materials](./materials.md) for more information.

## Train Deliveries

Materials obtained through the Company Train are delivered as part of the Company's logistics system.

Once a delivery is ready, Company members can collect the materials and use them towards the Company's current activities.

The train therefore provides an alternative way of obtaining resources without having to produce every required material directly.
