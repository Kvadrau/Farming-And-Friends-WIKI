# Roles & Permissions

Company Roles & Permissions determine what each member of a Company is allowed to do.

The Company Owner can assign members different roles, allowing responsibilities to be shared between trusted members.

## Company Roles

Each Company member has a role that determines their available permissions.

| Role | Description |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Owner | The player who created the Company and has full control. |
| Manager | A trusted member who can manage parts of the Company. |
| Member | A standard Company member who can participate in Company activities. |

> **Note:** The available roles and permissions may change as the Company system is updated.

## Owner

The **Owner** is the player who created the Company.

The Owner has the highest level of access and is responsible for managing the Company.

### Owner Permissions

The Owner can:

- Manage Company members
- Assign Company roles
- Change member permissions
- Manage Company settings
- Manage Company Orders
- Manage Company activities
- Remove members from the Company

The Owner should only give management permissions to trusted members.

## Admin

Admins are trusted Company members who can assist the Owner with running the Company.

### Admin Permissions

Admins may be able to:

- Manage Company members
- Assist with Company Orders
- Manage Company activities
- Help organise Company resources
- Perform other tasks granted by the Owner

## Member

Members are standard Company players.

They can participate in Company activities without having full management access.

### Member Permissions

Members can generally:

- View Company information
- View available Orders
- Gather Materials
- Complete Company tasks
- Prepare Shipments
- Contribute towards Company progression
- Earn Company rewards

Members cannot normally change important Company settings or manage other members unless additional permissions are granted.

## Managing Permissions

Company permissions should be assigned carefully.

Giving a player additional permissions allows them to perform Company management tasks that are not available to standard members.

The Owner should consider giving management permissions only to players they trust.

## Roles & Company Progression

Company roles do not prevent members from contributing to Company progression.

All members can contribute by participating in Company activities such as:

- Completing Orders
- Gathering Materials
- Preparing Shipments
- Using Company equipment
- Working towards seasonal progression

## Useful Tips

- Only give management permissions to trusted members.
- Use roles to divide responsibilities between Company members.
- Keep standard members on the lowest required permission level.
- Review Company members regularly.
- Remove inactive members when necessary.
- Make sure members understand what their role allows them to do.
- Use trusted Managers to help run larger Companies.

> **Note:** Company roles and permissions may be changed or expanded in future Seasons.
