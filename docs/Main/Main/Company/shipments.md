# Company Shipments

Company Shipments are used to deliver completed Company Orders.

After the required materials have been gathered and an Order has been fulfilled, the completed Order must be shipped to contribute towards Company progression.

## How Shipments Work

Company Shipments follow the final stage of the Company Order process:

1. Select an available Company Order.
2. Gather the required Materials.
3. Fulfil the Order.
4. Prepare the completed Order for shipment.
5. Load the shipment onto the required transport.
6. Deliver the shipment.
7. Receive the associated Company progression and rewards.
