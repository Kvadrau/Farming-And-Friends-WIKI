# Company Season 1

**Season 1** was the first Season of the **Companies** system in Farming And Friends.

Season 1 introduced limited Company equipment that could be obtained through Company progression.

## Season Overview

Season 1 introduced the first set of Company-exclusive equipment.

The equipment was designed to provide significantly wider working areas than standard equipment, making it particularly useful for players progressing through the Companies system.

## Season 1 Equipment

Season 1 introduced three pieces of limited farming equipment:

| Equipment | Type | Level | Working Width | Seed Capacity |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- | ------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| ZC3 | Plow | Level 5 | 4 Tiles | N/A |
| WRXL2 | Cultivator | Level 5 | 4 Tiles | N/A |
| GFS10 | Seeder | Level 5 | 4 Tiles | 800–1,300 Seeds |

All three pieces of equipment are listed as **Limited Equipment** and were associated with **Season 1 of Companies**.

## ZC3

The **ZC3** is a Level 5 plow introduced as part of Season 1.

### What It Does

The ZC3 is used to **plow fields**.

It works across **4 tiles at once**, making it considerably wider than many standard plows.

| Information | Details |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Equipment | ZC3 |
| Type | Plow |
| Level | Level 5 |
| Working Width | 4 Tiles |
| Availability | Limited |
| Season | Season 1 |

### Useful Information

- Requires a tractor capable of using Level 5 equipment.
- Plows **4 tiles** at a time.
- It is wider than standard lower-level plows.
- It was introduced as limited Company equipment during Season 1.

## WRXL2

The **WRXL2** is a Level 5 cultivator introduced as part of Season 1.

### What It Does

The WRXL2 is used to **cultivate fields** after they have been plowed.

It cultivates **4 tiles at once**, allowing large fields to be prepared more quickly.

| Information | Details |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Equipment | WRXL2 |
| Type | Cultivator |
| Level | Level 5 |
| Working Width | 4 Tiles |
| Availability | Limited |
| Season | Season 1 |

### Useful Information

- Requires a tractor capable of using Level 5 equipment.
- Cultivates **4 tiles** at a time.
- Designed for large-scale field preparation.
- It was introduced as limited Company equipment during Season 1.

## GFS10

The **GFS10** is a Level 5 seeder introduced as part of Season 1.

### What It Does

The GFS10 is used to **plant crops**.

It plants **4 tiles at once** and has a seed capacity of **800–1,300 seeds**.

| Information | Details |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Equipment | GFS10 |
| Type | Seeder |
| Level | Level 5 |
| Working Width | 4 Tiles |
| Seed Capacity | 800–1,300 Seeds |
| Availability | Limited |
| Season | Season 1 |

### Useful Information

- Requires a tractor capable of using Level 5 equipment.
- Seeds **4 tiles** at a time.
- Holds between **800 and 1,300 seeds**.
- Its large working width makes it useful for large-scale farming.
- It was introduced as limited Company equipment during Season 1.
