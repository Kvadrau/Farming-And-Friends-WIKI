# Company Season 2

**Season 2** was the second Season of the **Companies** system in Farming And Friends.

Season 2 introduced new limited equipment that could be obtained as part of the Company progression system.

## Season Overview

Season 2 expanded the selection of Company-exclusive equipment with new machinery designed for large-scale farming.

The documented Season 2 equipment includes:

- **ML10** — Level 5 Harvester
- **CW26** — Level 5 Sprinkler

Both pieces of equipment provide a **4-tile working width**, making them useful for large-scale farming.

## Season 2 Equipment

| Equipment | Type | Level | Working Width | Capacity |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- | ------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| ML10 | Harvester | Level 5 | 4 Tiles | 600–1,100 |
| CW26 | Sprinkler | Level 5 | 4 Tiles | N/A |

> **Note:** Both pieces of equipment are associated with Season 2 of Companies. Availability may change after the Season ends.

## ML10

The **ML10** is a Level 5 crop harvester introduced during Season 2 of Companies.

### What It Does

The ML10 is used to **harvest crops**.

It can harvest **4 tiles at once**, allowing large areas of farmland to be harvested more quickly than smaller harvesters.

### Useful Information
