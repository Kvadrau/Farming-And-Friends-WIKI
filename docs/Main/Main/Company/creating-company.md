# Creating a Company

Companies allow players to work together and complete Company Orders as a team.

Players can create their own Company and become its **Owner**, giving them control over the Company's members, roles, and permissions.

## Creation Costs

Creating a Company requires Coins.

| Information | Details |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Company Creation Cost | **[Confirm in-game] Coins** |
| Additional Cost | None currently confirmed |

> **Note:** The Company creation price may be changed by Dunn Games in future updates or Seasons.

## Creating a Company

To create a Company:

1. Open the **Company** menu.
2. Select **Create Company**.
3. Enter a name for your Company.
4. Confirm the Company creation requirements.
5. Pay the required creation cost.
6. Confirm the creation.

Once created, you become the **Company Owner**.

## Company Name

The Company Owner chooses the name of the Company when it is created.

The name is used to identify the Company to other players and its members.

> **Tip:** Choose a name that is easy for your members to recognise, especially if you plan on recruiting multiple players.

## Company Owner

The player who creates the Company becomes its **Owner**.

The Owner has the highest level of control over the Company and can manage Company members and permissions.

The Owner can also assign roles to other members where supported.

For more information about Company roles and permissions, see [Roles & Permissions](./permissions.md).

## Joining a Company

Players who do not want to create their own Company can join an existing Company.

A Company Owner or member with the appropriate permission can invite other players to join.

Once accepted, the player becomes a Company member and can participate in Company activities according to their assigned permissions.

## Company Progression

Creating a Company is only the beginning of the Company progression system.

After creating a Company, members can work together to:

- Complete Company Orders
- Gather required Materials
- Complete Shipments
- Earn Company rewards
- Progress through the current Season
- Work towards Company equipment and other rewards

## Useful Tips

- Make sure you have enough Coins before attempting to create a Company.
- Consider creating a Company with players you regularly play with.
- Assign appropriate permissions to trusted members.
- Keep track of the current Company Season, as requirements and rewards may change.
- Coordinate Company members when completing larger Orders.
- New members can contribute towards Company progression after joining.

> **Note:** Company features are subject to change as new Seasons and updates are released.
