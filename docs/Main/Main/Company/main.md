# Companies

Companies are a major part of **Farming And Friends**, allowing players to work together, complete Orders, manage resources, and progress as a team.

A Company can have multiple members, with different roles and permissions determining what each member is able to do.

Company progression is centred around completing **Orders**, gathering **Materials**, preparing **Shipments**, and working towards Company rewards and equipment.

## Company Requirements

Players must meet the requirements of the current Company system before they can create or participate in a Company.

Requirements may include:

- Access to the Company system
- The required Coins
- Available Company space
- Any requirements introduced by the current Season

> **Note:** Company requirements may change between Seasons.

## Creating a Company

Players can create their own Company and become its **Owner**.

The Company Owner is responsible for managing the Company and its members.

### Creation Costs

Creating a Company requires Coins.

The exact creation cost may depend on the current version of the Companies system.

> **Note:** Check the in-game Company menu for the current creation cost.

## Roles & Permissions

Companies allow members to have different roles and permissions.

The Owner has the highest level of control over the Company, while standard members can participate in Company activities without having full management access.

| Role | Description |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Owner | The player who created the Company and has the highest level of control. |
| Manager | A trusted member who can assist with managing the Company. |
| Member | A standard Company member who can participate in Company activities. |

Permissions determine which Company functions each member can access.

> **Note:** Roles and permissions may change as the Companies system is updated.

## Orders

**Company Orders** are tasks that Companies complete as part of the Company progression system.

Orders require the Company to gather specific Materials and fulfil the requirements before they can be shipped.
