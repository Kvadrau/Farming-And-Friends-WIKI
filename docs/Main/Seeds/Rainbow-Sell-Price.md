# 🌈 Rainbow Price Chart

This page contains the **Rainbow values** for seeds in **Farming & Friends**.

Rainbow values are the amount received for each crop, log, or plank when sold at Rainbow quality.

> **Note:** Values may change when the game is updated. If you notice an incorrect value, please let us know through the official Discord server.
---
:::tip
all growth times on this base are with the base equipment, at level 5 equipment you can speed growth up by a maximum of 2x!!! turning what would be 40 minutes to wait for potatos into only 20 minutes!
:::

| Seed | Normal Box Price | Rainbow Log Value | Rainbow Plank Value | Growth Time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 🌲Pine | 600 | 57 per log | — | 12 min |
| 🌴Cocoa | 70,000 | 1,825 per log | — | 32 min |
| 🌳Cypress | 150,000 | 4,750 per log | 9,750 per plank| 38 min |
| 🌳Birch | 210,000 | 7,000 per log | 14,000 per plank| 55 min |

> **Cocoa:** Cocoa can also be used to make Chocolate Milk.

> **Cypress & Birch:** These trees can be processed into planks. A Sawmill License is required to turn logs into planks.
