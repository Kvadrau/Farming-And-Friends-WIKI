### Step By Step Instructions

1. **Step 1:** To begin harvesting you must be inside of a harvester, you can click the drive button while next to the harvester to enter it.
2. **Step 2:** You then need to drive forward within the field to harvest the drop. (RT- console) , (W - Keyboard) , (Big Right pedal - Mobile)
::: tip
Make sure the field has the Harvest icon above it before trying to harvest
:::

### Step By Step Tutorial

<br>

1. **Step 1:** Bring your harvester next to a crop trailer so you can begin to offload your harvest.
2. **Step 2:** To offload your harvest you need to unfold it by pressing (R - PC) , (Right Screen Pour Button - mobile) , ( LB - Console)
3. **Step 3:** Whilst the harvester is next to the crop trailer it will automatically dispose its inventory into the crop trailer.
