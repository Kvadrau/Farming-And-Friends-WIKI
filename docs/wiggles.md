# Tractors

Tractors are one of the main types of vehicles in **Farming And Friends**. They are primarily used for agricultural work, including plowing, cultivating and planting fields.

Tractors are available across multiple levels, with higher-level tractors generally offering improved road and field speeds. Some tractors are also obtained through events, promotions, starter packs, Companies or other limited-time methods.

## Tractor Information

Each tractor has two listed speed values:

| Statistic | Description |
|---|---|
| **Road Speed** | The tractor's maximum speed when travelling on roads and other non-field surfaces. |
| **Field Speed** | The tractor's maximum speed while operating on a field. |

Speed values are displayed out of **100**, with higher values representing faster vehicles.

> **Note:** Actual vehicle performance may vary depending on terrain, equipment being towed and other gameplay conditions.

## Using Tractors

Tractors can be used with a variety of farming equipment. Depending on the equipment, a tractor can be used to:

- Plow fields
- Cultivate fields
- Plant crops
- Tow trailers
- Transport equipment
- Perform forestry-related tasks
- Complete farming tasks

When purchasing a tractor, consider both its **road speed** and **field speed**, as well as the equipment you intend to use with it.

> **Tip:** A tractor with a high road speed is useful for travelling around the map, while a high field speed can reduce the time spent working large fields.
