Chickens ​

Chickens are a small livestock animal that produce Eggs. They are one of the cheapest production animals and are particularly useful early in the game and for supplying eggs to the Cake Factory.

How to Obtain ​

Chickens can be purchased from AnimalCo.

To purchase chickens:

  1. Take an animal trailer to AnimalCo.
  2. Position the trailer in the marked animal purchase area.
  3. Purchase the desired number of chickens.
  4. The chickens will be loaded into the trailer.
  5. Transport them back to your farm.
  6. Place them into a suitable fenced area with access to a Chicken Coop and Small Feeder.

Chickens are small animals, meaning they can be transported using any suitable animal trailer.

Purchase Price ​

InformationDetails
Purchase Price1,500 Coins per Chicken

Each chicken costs 1,500 Coins when purchased from AnimalCo.

Housing Requirements ​

Chickens require a fenced area and a Chicken Coop in order to produce Eggs.

There are two types of coop available:

HousingDetails
Chicken CoopStandard chicken housing
Medium Chicken CoopHolds twice as many chickens as the regular Chicken Coop

Chickens must be able to access their coop and feeder.

The coop also needs to be cleaned regularly to maintain efficient egg production.

Food Requirements ​

Chickens eat Animal Food.

They require a Small Feeder to provide them with food.

Animal Food can be purchased from AnimalCo or produced using an Animal Food Factory.

RequirementDetails
FoodAnimal Food
FeederSmall Feeder
Food SourceAnimalCo / Animal Food Factory

Important: Keep the feeder stocked with Animal Food so the chickens can continue producing Eggs.

Products Produced ​

Chickens produce Eggs.

Eggs are collected from the Chicken Coop once enough eggs have been produced.

ProductDetails
EggsProduced by Chickens
Egg BoxContains 12 Eggs

12 Eggs are required to produce one box of Eggs.

Eggs can be used in the following factories:

  • Cake Factory
  • Scrambled Egg Factory

Production Time ​

A fixed Egg production time is not currently reliably documented.

Eggs are produced relatively quickly, and the coop displays the current egg count so players can check when Eggs are ready to collect.

Once the coop reaches 12 Eggs, an Egg Box can be collected.

Note: A specific production time should be added once confirmed in-game rather than using an unverified value.

Selling Information ​

Eggs can be sold at AnimalCo.

Egg Boxes can be transported using a trailer, truck, or carried by hand.

ProductSelling Information
EggsSold at AnimalCo
Egg BoxContains 12 Eggs

Eggs can also be used as an ingredient rather than sold directly.

Using Eggs in the Cake Factory can make them useful as part of a larger production chain.

They are introduced early through the Animals Goals progression.

Other Useful Information ​

  • Chickens cost 1,500 Coins each.
  • Chickens are classified as small animals.
  • Chickens require a Chicken Coop or Medium Chicken Coop to produce Eggs.
  • The Medium Chicken Coop holds twice as many chickens as the standard Chicken Coop.
  • Chickens use a Small Feeder.
  • Chickens eat Animal Food.
  • Coops need to be cleaned regularly.
  • One Egg Box contains 12 Eggs.
  • Eggs can be used in the Cake Factory.
  • Eggs can also be used in the Scrambled Egg Factory.
  • Chickens are one of the cheapest production animals but have relatively low income compared with cows and other advanced production animals.
  • Chickens are useful for completing early Animals Goals, including collecting and selling Eggs.
  • Chickens can be purchased from AnimalCo and transported to your farm using an animal trailer.
