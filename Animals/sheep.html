Sheep ​

Sheep are a livestock animal that produce Wool. Wool can be collected by shearing sheep and processed using a Spindle Machine to create Wool Bundles, which can then be sold at AnimalCo.

How to Obtain ​

Sheep can be purchased from AnimalCo.

To purchase sheep:

  1. Take a suitable animal trailer to AnimalCo.
  2. Position the trailer in the marked animal purchase area.
  3. Purchase the desired number of sheep.
  4. The sheep will automatically be loaded into the trailer.
  5. Transport them back to your farm.

A trailer capable of transporting large animals is required.

Sheep cost 3,000 Coins each when purchased from AnimalCo.

Housing Requirements ​

Sheep require a suitable fenced area where they can roam.

RequirementDetails
Fenced AreaRequired
FeederRegular Feeder
Animal FoodRequired
BarnNot required

Sheep use a Regular Feeder rather than a Chicken Feeder. They also need enough space to move around and access their food.

Food Requirements ​

Sheep eat Animal Food.

Animal Food can be purchased from AnimalCo or produced using an Animal Food Factory.

To feed sheep, place a Regular Feeder in their fenced area and fill it with Animal Food.

Important: Sheep need to remain fed in order to continue producing Wool.

Products Produced ​

Sheep produce Wool.

Wool can be collected by shearing sheep and then processed using a Spindle Machine to create Wool Bundles.

ProductDetails
WoolCollected by shearing Sheep
Wool BundleProduced using a Spindle Machine
Wool Bundle UseUsed in the Shirt Factory

The current 2026 community guide lists Wool Bundles as requiring 5 pieces of Wool.

Production Time ​

A fixed Wool production time is not currently reliably documented in the available sources.

Wool production is tied to the sheep's wool growth cycle. Once Wool is available, the sheep can be sheared manually.

Note: A fixed production time should be added once confirmed in-game rather than using an unverified value.

Selling Information ​

Sheep themselves can be sold for 1,500 Coins each.

Wool is processed into Wool Bundles, which can be sold at the animal product selling area at AnimalCo.

Wool can also be used as an ingredient in the Shirt Factory, making sheep useful for textile production.

Required License ​

LicenseRequired
Sheep LicenseYes

The Sheep License is required before Sheep can be purchased.

The Sheep License is unlocked through the Animals Goals progression at approximately 7,200 XP.

Other Useful Information ​

  • Sheep cost 3,000 Coins each.
  • Sheep require a Sheep License.
  • Sheep require a fenced area, Regular Feeder and Animal Food.
  • Sheep can be manually sheared using Shears purchased from AnimalCo.
  • Wool can be processed into Wool Bundles using a Spindle Machine.
  • Wool Bundles can be sold at AnimalCo.
  • Wool is also used in the Shirt Factory.
  • A suitable animal trailer is required to transport Sheep from AnimalCo to your farm.
  • The default animal limit is 32 animals per farm, increased to 52 with the More Animals game pass.
  • Keeping Sheep fed is important for maintaining Wool production.
