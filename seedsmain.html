Seeds ​

There are so many seeds in farming and friends. Some can be obtained through simply going to the local store and buying some, wheras others may be obtained through events or even being abducted by aliens! Eather way, they are one of the best ways of making money in the game and can also look very cool in a farm!

Perminant Seeds ​

There are 2 main types of seeds in farming and friends, crops & trees. Crops can simply be harvevested once finished growing and require to be plowed, cultivated, and seeded.(Exept for grapes, they dont require plows or cultivators.)Crops also require regular soil.(Again, Grapes use a different field type being the trallis.)Where as trees are grown on tree soil and are cut down by ither a player or with a tree cutter vehicle. They also require to have their fields plowed cultivated and drilled.
