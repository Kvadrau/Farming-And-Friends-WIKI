Want to Contribute? ​

You don't need to be part of the Wiki team to contribute.

If you have information that could help improve the Farming And Friends Wiki, please contact the Wiki team.

Useful contributions include:

  • New or updated information
  • Screenshots
  • Videos demonstrating mechanics
  • Corrections to existing pages
  • Information about new updates
  • Missing item or vehicle information
  • Event information
  • Confirmed prices and requirements

Note: All submitted information may be checked and verified before being added to the Wiki.
