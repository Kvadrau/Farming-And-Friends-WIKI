Introduction to F&F ​

Welcome to the Beginner's Guide! This guide is designed to help new players understand the basics of the game and get started on the right path.

Starting a new game can sometimes feel confusing, especially when there are many mechanics, items, and systems to learn. This guide aims to simplify things and walk you through the most important parts step by step.

What This Guide Covers ​

Within this guide, you will find information about:

  • The basic gameplay mechanics
  • How to start progressing efficiently
  • Important features every new player should know
  • Tips to avoid common beginner mistakes
  • Helpful strategies to make early progression easier

By the end of this guide, you should have a solid understanding of how the game works in the early stages and feel confident continuing your journey.
