Juicery ​

The Juicery is a production building that converts Harvested Grape Crop into Grape Juice.

Grape Juice can be sold at the liquid selling location at the middle of the map.

The Juicery is part of the Juicery License which is needed to place the Juicery on your plot and get started!

The Juicery takes Grape Crop as the only input.

There is no capacity limit to input or output of the Juicery.

Production Time ​

The Juicery does not currently have a reliably documented fixed production time in the available community documentations.

Production is handled through the factory's internal production process rather than simple crop-style growth timer.

Note: Production time may vary depending on the current version of the game. This section should be updated if an exact in-game product time is confirmed.

Products Produced ​

The Juicery produces Grape Juice.

1 Grape Juice requires 5 Grape Crop.

Selling Price ​

Grape Juice can be sold at the Liquid section of the Central Selling Point.

The current community documentation lists the maximum price at approximately 15,625 Coins per unit of Grape Juice.

For example:

50 × 15,625 = 781,250 Coins

Therefore, 50 Grape Juice yields approximately 781,250 Coins.

Note: Selling prices can change with game updates, so verify the current in-game price before treating this as a permanent value.

To operate and transport Grape Juice efficiently, you will need:

  • Juicery License

  • Grape Equipment

    • Grape Seeder

  • Suitable vehicle to hitch Crop Trailer

How to Use the Juicery ​

1. Grow Grapes ​

Place a vineyard of suitable size and plant it with Grape Seeds using the Moped + Grape Seeder.

2. Harvest Grapes ​

Once the grapes are fully grown, use the Moped + Grape Harvester to collect the grapes.

3. Unlock Juicery License ​

Unlock the Juicery License under Farming Goal.

4. Load the Juicery ​

Use a trailer or directly deposit Grape Crop into the input of the Juicery.

5. Collect the Grape Juice ​

Use an appropriate liquid trailer/truck to load Grape Juice from the output of the factory.

6. Transport & Sell Grape Juice ​

Once loaded into an appropriate liquid trailer/truck, transport the Grape Juice to the Liquid Sell Point in the center of the map.
