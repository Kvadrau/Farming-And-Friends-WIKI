Pizza Factory ​

The Pizza Factory is a production building used to turn farming and animal products into Pizza.

Pizza is part of the game's production system, requiring multiple ingredients to be gathered and processed before the final product can be produced and sold.

How to Use the Factory ​

1. Unlock the Pizza Factory ​

Before producing Pizza, make sure you have unlocked the required license and building through the game's progression system.

The exact license requirement is currently not confirmed.

2. Produce the Required Ingredients ​

Grow and collect the ingredients required for Pizza production.

Tomatoes are one of the documented crops used by the Pizza Factory.

Other ingredients should be prepared before starting production once the complete recipe is confirmed.

3. Load the Factory ​

Transport the required ingredients to the Pizza Factory.

Place the ingredients into the appropriate loading area.

The exact recipe quantities should be confirmed before production begins.

4. Start Production ​

Once enough ingredients have been supplied, activate the factory's production process.

Note: The exact Pizza Factory production procedure should be documented once the current in-game mechanics have been confirmed.

5. Collect the Pizza ​

Once production has finished, collect the finished Pizza from the factory's output area.

6. Transport and Sell ​

Load the Pizza onto a suitable trailer or transport vehicle and take it to a selling location.

The current selling value should be checked in-game before calculating profits.
